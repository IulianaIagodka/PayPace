Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

PayPace (“we”, “the app”) is a payday-to-payday budgeting app. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and how you can control it.

1. Summary

  • Budget data on your device stays on your device by default.
  • Optional features may send limited data to third-party services you enable (receipt scan, shared budget, in-app purchases).
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your content for advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

PayPace stores your budget settings, pay cycles, bills, expenses, category allocations, and related preferences locally on your phone (for example via on-device storage). This data is used only to run the budgeting features you see in the app.

3. Camera and photos (receipt scan)

If you choose Photo receipt, the app asks for camera and/or photo library permission. Images are used only to extract expense line items (merchant, amounts, categories). You control when a photo is taken or selected.

When AI receipt scanning is enabled for your build, the selected image (or a derived representation) is sent to OpenAI to parse the receipt. Do not photograph sensitive documents you do not want processed by that service. See OpenAI’s privacy policy.

4. Bank statement import

If you import a bank export (for example CSV), the file is read on your device to create expense entries. We do not upload statement files to our own servers. Parsing happens in the app; unsupported formats are rejected rather than replaced with fake data.

5. Shared budget (optional, Plus)

If you create or join a shared household budget, sync may use Supabase (or equivalent backend configured for your build) to store household membership, invite codes, and shared cycle / expense data so a partner device can stay in sync. Only participants you invite should have access to that shared budget.

See Supabase privacy for their processing terms.

6. Purchases (Plus)

Plus purchases and restores are handled by Apple (App Store) and, when configured, RevenueCat for entitlement status. We receive purchase/entitlement signals needed to unlock Plus features—not your full payment card details.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, PayPace may show local alerts (for example when shared-budget metrics change). Notification permission is optional and can be changed in system Settings.

8. Analytics and tracking

The current PayPace builds described by this policy do not include a separate advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. If that changes, this page will be updated before those features ship.

9. Children

PayPace is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

10. Your choices

  • Deny camera/photos permissions — receipt scan will not work.
  • Stay on Free — shared sync and Plus cloud features stay off.
  • Leave a shared household to stop partner sync for that budget.
  • Delete the app to remove on-device PayPace storage on that device.
  • Manage App Store subscriptions / purchases in Apple ID settings.

11. Data retention

On-device data remains until you delete it in-app (where available) or remove the app. Shared-budget server data persists while the household exists and according to the backend project configuration. Receipt images sent to OpenAI are processed under OpenAI’s retention practices.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: Support or email iuliana.iagodka@gmail.com (update this address if you prefer another contact).

13. Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
